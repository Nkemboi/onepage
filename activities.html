Activities to do

Stay close to the wild and enjoy nature’s beauty — Mombasa, Diani, and a Kilimanjaro week. Tap a card; the story opens here.

Mombasa

Snorkel with the dolphins

Snorkel with the dolphins and explore vibrant marine life. Discover the coral reefs and unforgettable underwater scenery. Afterward, relax on the beaches or indulge in local cuisine.

  • Reef snorkeling
  • Boat from the Mombasa / Diani coast
  • Optional lunch on shore

Mombasa

Haller Park

Interact with giraffes, hippos and other wildlife in Haller Park. A guided tour covers the conservation work, lush trails, and the animals that now live on the old quarry.

  • Guided walk
  • Giraffe and hippo viewing
  • Easy half-day from town

Mombasa

Cuisine treasures of Mombasa

Experience the culinary delights of Mombasa — rich spices, fresh ingredients, street food from samosas to coconut rice, and the culture that sits behind every plate.

  • Old Town and street food
  • Swahili lunch or dinner
  • Market walk

Mombasa

Fort Jesus Museum

Fort Jesus, in Mombasa, is a repository of coastal history. Artifacts, coral-stone architecture, and guided tours that go into the stories of the port.

  • UNESCO fort
  • Guided museum visit
  • Old Town on the same morning

Mombasa

Nguuni Nature Sanctuary

Peaceful wildlife encounters at Nguuni Nature Sanctuary. Walking trails, giraffes, and birds in a sanctuary that still feels like bush, not a zoo.

  • Walking trails
  • Birding
  • Half-day from Mombasa

6 nights

Scaling Mount Kilimanjaro

A 6-night adventure on Mount Kilimanjaro. Breathtaking views, diverse ecosystems, and the chance to stand on the roof of Africa with a team we trust.

  • Six nights on the mountain
  • Porters and guides arranged
  • Pairs with Amboseli before or after

Coast

Visit to Diani Beach

Diani Beach — turquoise ocean, soft white sand, water sports, shoreline food, and the local culture of the south coast.

  • Beach day or overnight
  • Water sports on request
  • Easy after Tsavo

Coast

Boat trips & jet skis

A glass-bottom boat over the reef, and jet skiing for an additional fee. Coastline views, marine life under the hull, coves and coral.

  • Glass-bottom boat
  • Optional jet ski
  • Half-day from Diani or Mombasa

Mombasa

Art gallery & Jambo Beach

The Mombasa Art Gallery for local work, then Jambo Beach for live music and bonfires. A stroll on the shore when the air cools.

  • Gallery visit
  • Evening on the beach
  • Best as a city night
