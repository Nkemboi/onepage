Important information

The practical things that make Kenya feel easy — entry, health, money, and how we move in the parks.

Entry & eTA

Most visitors need an approved Electronic Travel Authorisation before they fly. Kenya no longer issues visas on arrival. Apply on the official eCitizen portal and keep a copy on your phone and in print. East African Community citizens follow a separate, simpler rule.

  • Passport valid at least six months beyond entry, with two blank pages
  • eTA approved before check-in — airlines will ask
  • Stay is typically up to 90 days per entry

Health

Yellow fever proof is required if you are arriving from, or have transited, a yellow-fever endemic country. Direct flights from Europe, North America or much of Asia usually do not need it for entry, but a certificate is still wise if you may continue to Tanzania or Uganda.

Malaria is present in most safari and coastal areas, including the Mara, Tsavo, Amboseli and the coast. Nairobi and the highlands are lower risk. Speak to a travel clinic four to six weeks before departure about prophylaxis, Hepatitis A and typhoid. There is no COVID test or vaccine rule for entry in 2026.

  • Start malaria tablets as prescribed — do not skip them in the parks
  • DEET repellent, long sleeves after dusk, and travel insurance with evacuation
  • Drink bottled or filtered water; ice in reputable lodges is fine

Money & practicalities

The shilling (KES) is the local currency. US dollars in small, recent notes are useful for tips. Cards work in Nairobi and larger lodges; carry some cash for parks, markets and the coast. ATMs are reliable in cities.

  • Power is Type G, 240V — bring a UK-style adaptor
  • Time is East Africa Time, UTC+3, with no daylight saving
  • We drive on the left. Park game drives are always with a licensed guide

In the parks

Stay in the vehicle unless your guide says otherwise. Do not feed wildlife. Ask before photographing people, especially in villages and at markets. Soft earth tones photograph well and keep you cooler than black.

  • Tip guides and lodge staff in USD or KES at the end of the stay
  • Park fees are usually included when we quote — we will say if not
  • Dust is part of Tsavo and the Mara; a scarf and zip bags help
